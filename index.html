Bring Your Signature Weave to Life

Just like a custom-cut gemstone, extraordinary collections begin with a material created exclusively for you. Partner with our master textile engineers to custom-mill colors, pile heights, and weights.

01

Share Your Inspiration & Pantone

Send us your moodboard, Pantone TCX code, or a physical reference swatch along with your target GSM and width.

02

Complimentary Lab-Dip & Strike-Off

Within 5–7 days, our atelier prepares custom color lab-dips and tactile hand-loom samples for your approval.

03

Precision Milling & Global Delivery

Once approved, your custom fabric is ethically milled with multi-point inspection and shipped directly to your production house.
